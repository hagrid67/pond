Privacy and GDPR Policy

Highgate Men's Pond Association (HMPA) | Website: ponds.nsupdate.info | Last updated: 2026-07-29

1. Who We Are

Highgate Men's Pond Association (HMPA) is a small sports user group. This policy explains how we handle personal data in relation to our member records and pond status update services.

2. Data We Process

  • Account data you provide on the pond website, such as nickname, email address (if provided), and password hash.
  • Email verification records for account confirmation links.
  • Pond status updates submitted through ponds.nsupdate.info, including optional account linkage if you are logged in.
  • Member list data held in MemberMojo, including email addresses used to identify possible member status in our pond service.
  • Basic technical logs needed for security, reliability, and troubleshooting.

3. Why We Process Data

  • To operate login and registration features.
  • To verify email ownership and secure accounts.
  • To flag whether an account email appears in the latest MemberMojo membership export.
  • To run pond status features and improve service reliability.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

4. Lawful Bases

Depending on context, processing is carried out under one or more GDPR lawful bases:

  • Legitimate interests (running member communication and community services).
  • Consent (where requested, for optional communications or optional data fields).
  • Legal obligation (where required by law).

5. Data Sharing and Processors

  • MemberMojo acts as a separate platform for membership administration.
  • Infrastructure and hosting providers may process limited data on our behalf.
  • We do not sell personal data.

6. Retention

  • Account records are retained while an account is active, and for a limited period afterwards for security and audit needs.
  • Email verification tokens are retained only for operational/security audit periods.
  • Pond status submissions are retained for operational and reporting purposes, with periodic review.
  • MemberMojo retention is governed by HMPA membership administration practice in that platform.

7. Your GDPR Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification: request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: request deletion of your data where applicable.
  • Restriction: request processing restriction in certain cases.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Portability: request data in a portable format where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

8. Security

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to a small volunteer-run service, including access control, password hashing, and operational logging for abuse prevention and incident response.

9. Contact and Requests

For GDPR requests (data copy, correction, deletion, objection), contact HMPA using the association contact route published on the HMPA website or official communications channel. We aim to respond within one month, subject to lawful extensions.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The latest version is published on this page.
