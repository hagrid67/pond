Highgate Men's Pond Booking Stats (Beta)

Highgate Men's Pond Association (HMPA) is reporting on the availability of tickets for the bookable swimming sessions. We also provided real-time temperatures for the pond for a few months in 2024 and 2025, but the City of London removed the sensor in April 2025. The real-time temperatures here are now being sourced from a (very) small garden pond, as a proxy. Its temperature varies much more than the pond.
